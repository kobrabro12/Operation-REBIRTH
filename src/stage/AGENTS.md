# src/stage/ — stages

Every stage: layout, art, hazards (and their tunables), music, how it is unlocked.
The root `AGENTS.md` (rules, stack, map) applies too. Keep this file up to date when you change what it describes.

## BOSS ARENAS
Files: `bossBase.js` (the shared boss engine: `BossArena`), `steamBossStage.js`, `polyBossStage.js`, `demonBossStage.js`; bosses / difficulties / rewards in `src/data/bosses.js`; menus in src/scenes (BossFightsScene / BossRewardScene); music `bgm_boss_colossus|polygon|demon` from `tools/audio/boss_music.py`.

- A boss arena is an ordinary stage (platforms, art, a `HazardCycle`) that extends `BossArena`. In a boss fight (Trinkets > BOSS FIGHTS: MatchScene `create(data)` with `data.boss = { id, tier, setup }`, setup = the usual match settings - one player, stocks from the difficulty, no items) MatchScene calls `stage.startBoss(match, { boss, tier, T })`; the arena's `step` calls `bossStep` (pauses, attack picks from `pattern(phase)`, `start_<atk>` / `atk_<atk>(match, a, out)` returning true when done; ENRAGED at half health: `onRage`, `rageLine`), its `render()` (MatchScene.draw calls `stage.render` when a stage has one) draws the boss every frame and `drawBar()` its health bar. The boss is hit through `strikeTargets` against its `hurt()` regions (`mult`: weak points > 1); its attacks are hazard hitboxes from `bhit(id, box, dmg, [angle, base, growth])` (unblockable, scaled by the difficulty's `dmg`; growth written small and x`GROWTH`); `sp(n)` shortens wind-ups by the difficulty's `speed`. Health gone -> `startDeath` -> `match.bossDefeated()` (a win; MatchScene then goes to BossReward via `toBossReward`, which pays out with `claimBossRewards`); every player out -> a loss. `camPoints()` keeps the boss in the camera; `match.bossProxy` is what the CPU (`cpu.js pickTarget`) goes for in the sim (`npm run sim -- --boss id[:tier]`, `bossFight.cpu`).
- Out of a boss fight (opened for VS by a HARD/NIGHTMARE win: `bossArena` in stages.js, `stageLocked` reads `rebirth.bosses` directly) there's no `boss`: only the stage and its hazards, the boss shown as scenery.
- BRASS FOUNDRY (`brass-foundry`, THE CLOCKWORK COLOSSUS, steampunk): brick foundry, arched windows onto a dusk sky, wall gears (`drawGears`), pipes, gauges, lamps; iron deck with brass trim and vent grates (`VENT.xs`), two catwalks. Hazards PIPE BURST (`PIPE`, `jet`) and CRANE DROP (`CRANE`, `crate`). In VS it stands slumped and dark.

The King is built from faces every frame (`kingFaces`, `xf` = turn + place faces): faceted head with diamond eyes, brows, a dropping jaw and a crown; two gem gauntlets (`HAND` scale). Head x1 (x1.5 in a FRAME DROP: every `DROP.every`-th attack he glitches and sinks), gauntlets x0.4 (x0.6 resting). Attacks `PALM`, `SWIPE`, `GUN` (a gauntlet becomes a finger gun - `mode: 'gun'` in `kingFaces` - aims by `roll`, kicks, fires `shots`; ENRAGED both hands), `GOON` (POLY GOONS: warp rings, then little cube minions that waddle after you and hop - `stepGoons`, `goonFaces`; players smash them through the overridden `strikeTargets`), `RAIN` (cubes, `stepCubes`), `BEAM` (ENRAGED eye beam). - THE BURNING KEEP (`burning-keep`, KARNAX, THE MAD BLADE, medieval demon; `demonBossStage.js`): night sky with a red moon, black mountains and the burning town (`paintSky`, placed by `SKY` on the background camera), the battlement walk, two towers with banners and braziers, torches (`fire()`). - THE INTRO (every boss, `bossBase.js`): MatchScene runs `startIntro` / `introStep` before the countdown (not in the sim): `INTRO.enter` frames of the arena's `introPose(match, k, t)` (Colossus: rises dark from under the deck, eyes ignite, fists up; King: head drops spinning and bounces, gauntlets fly in and become finger guns; KARNAX: rises out of a fire pillar laughing, swings into his pose), then `INTRO.freeze` frames frozen on the pose with `drawIntro` (flash, letterbox, speed lines, a slanted band with `short` + `title` from bosses.js) and the camera pushed in (`introCam`, aimed by the arena's `introAt`). J / Enter / Space / pad A / a click skips (`skipIntro`); `endIntro` shows the health bar.
