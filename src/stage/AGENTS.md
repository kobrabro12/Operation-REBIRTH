# src/stage/ — stages

Every stage: layout, art, hazards (and their tunables), music, how it is unlocked.
The root `AGENTS.md` (rules, stack, map) applies too. Keep this file up to date when you change what it describes.

## BOSS ARENAS
Files: `bossBase.js` (the shared boss engine: `BossArena`), `steamBossStage.js`, `polyBossStage.js`, `demonBossStage.js`, `knightBossStage.js`, `machineBossStage.js`, `smudgeBossStage.js`, `maestroBossStage.js`; bosses / difficulties / rewards in `src/data/bosses.js`; menus in src/scenes (BossFightsScene / BossRewardScene); music `bgm_boss_colossus|polygon|demon|knight|machine|smudge|maestro` from `tools/audio/boss_music.py`.

- A boss arena is an ordinary stage (platforms, art, a `HazardCycle`) that extends `BossArena`. In a boss fight (Trinkets > BOSS FIGHTS: MatchScene `create(data)` with `data.boss = { id, tier, setup }`, setup = the usual match settings - one player, stocks from the difficulty, no items) MatchScene calls `stage.startBoss(match, { boss, tier, T })`; the arena's `step` calls `bossStep` (pauses, attack picks from `pattern(phase)`, `start_<atk>` / `atk_<atk>(match, a, out)` returning true when done; ENRAGED at half health - or at the arena's `rageAt`: `onRage`, `rageLine`), its `render()` (MatchScene.draw calls `stage.render` when a stage has one) draws the boss every frame and `drawBar()` its health bar. The boss is hit through `strikeTargets` against its `hurt()` regions (`mult`: weak points > 1; `mult: 0` = armour that can't be hurt - a clang and sparks, `onArmor(match, x, y, owner)`; `onHurt(match, region, dmg)` after a real hit); its attacks are hazard hitboxes from `bhit(id, box, dmg, [angle, base, growth])` (unblockable, scaled by the difficulty's `dmg`; growth written small and x`GROWTH`); `sp(n)` shortens wind-ups by the difficulty's `speed`. Health gone -> `startDeath` -> `match.bossDefeated()` (BOSS DEFEATED! clears after `koBanner` frames so the death can be seen; MatchScene waits `deathLen()` + 70 before the results; while dying the camera stays wide on the arena - `camPoints`) (a win; MatchScene then goes to BossReward via `toBossReward`, which pays out with `claimBossRewards`); every player out -> a loss: the boss GLOATS (`bossTaunt`: one of its BOSSES `taunts` in big letters in its colour, a laugh, then the arena's `onGloat(match, t)` every frame - the Colossus raises its fists and blasts steam, the King cackles and claps, KARNAX laughs, THORNWARD's soul dances round his helm, BEIGE.EXE grins and waves its cursor); the line goes on to BossReward (`taunt`). `camPoints()` keeps the boss in the camera; `match.bossProxy` is what the CPU (`cpu.js pickTarget`) goes for in the sim (`npm run sim -- --boss id[:tier]`, `bossFight.cpu`).
- Out of a boss fight (opened for VS by a HARD/NIGHTMARE win: `bossArena` in stages.js, `stageLocked` reads `rebirth.bosses` directly) there's no `boss`: only the stage and its hazards, the boss shown as scenery.
