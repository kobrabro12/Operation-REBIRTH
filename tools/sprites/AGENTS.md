# tools/sprites/ — sprite generators

How fighter and costume art is generated from Mason's drawings.
The root `AGENTS.md` (rules, stack, map) applies too. Keep this file up to date when you change what it describes.

## THE DEVOTED
`devoted_draw.py`: THE DEVOTED's four sludge-stage looks (repaints AERO's parts; sludge coverage `COVER`, smile per stage in `head()`, `wing()` = DROWNED's demon wing in the scarf slot, her own `idle_keys`) and her moves table `src/fighters/dvMoves.js`. Rerun it after changing the drawing. `--set murder` builds the MAD MURDER costume instead (`VARIANT`: `MURDER_SHIRT`, `BLOOD`, `bloody()`, `mask()`, a red blade) into `devoted/murder/`. `--set cartridge|bride|ember` build the other drawn costumes: `VARIANTS` (palettes, `facet` = flat low-poly shading, sludge ramp, wing colours, blade) applied by `apply_variant()`, plus their own touches in `vpaint()` (clothes patterns), `vhead()` (eyes, veil, halo, fire hair), `dress()` (skirt, bouquet, rose, rosary) and `knife()`.

## AERO64
`aero_3d.py`: AERO's AERO64 costume as a real low-poly 3D model in Blender (needs `pip install bpy`), on top of devoted_3d.py (`Geo`, `palette_material`, `build_scene`, `animate3d`, `snap`, `shrink`; it swaps in its own `model()` and `place()`): glass fishbowl (`glass_material`: transparent middle, solid rim; the back of the bowl and the water are opaque so the outline doesn't punch holes), goldfish, suit, gloves, boots, leaf scarf; AERO's rig/keyframes/life from aero_moves.py -> `public/assets/fighters/aero/n64/` (moves `retile`d to 36 a row). Then `ONLY=aero_n64_moves python3 tools/sprites/mobile_atlases.py`.

## TROPHY HALL paintings
`hall_paintings.py` paints the 22 pictures in the TROPHY HALL (-> `public/assets/hall/paintings.png`, 5 to a row of 160x112, palette PNG; titles and stories in `PAINTINGS` in `src/data/trophyHall.js`): each `p_*` builds a background in code (Bayer-dithered sky bands `sky` / `bands`, `radial` glows, `ridge` silhouettes, rays, speed lines, impact `burst`s, sparkles) and stands the fighters in it from their real sheets (`strip` = a frame of an idle/run strip, `move` = a frame of a move, read from the generated `*Moves.js` tables; `put` trims and places by the feet, with a rim of the scene's light; `silhouette` for crowds/shadows), then `grade` (colour wash, contrast, vignette, grain). WHAT ONCE WAS uses `menu_idle_sheet.png` (its other sheets have no head). `--preview FILE` writes a 3x contact sheet. `hall_masterpieces.py` paints the four animated MASTERPIECES (320x224, 8 frames each -> `public/assets/hall/master_<name>.png`: `clash`, `feast`, `drowned`, `bowl`) on the same helpers (it sets `hall_paintings.W/H` to its size): nearer fighters at 2x, far ones at 1x for depth, clouds / waterfalls / embers / candles / steam / rain / lightning / waves moving with the frame number `f` (`ph(f)` = its phase), a fixed grain per piece (`finish`). `bowl` is a MOSAIC: `bowl_source(f)` paints a smooth picture of AERO's fishbowl, `tiles()` lays fixed Voronoi stones once (scipy `cKDTree`: square stones in the border, rings round the bowl), each stone takes the average colour under it with its own shade jitter, then a bevel, grout and glinting gold stones. Extra helpers in hall_paintings.py: `shop()` (a shopkeeper face or shop picture), `demon()` (THE DEVOTED's glutton / wraith / weeper, drawn small).

## Shop costumes
Files: `tools/sprites/shop_costumes.py` (+ helpers `costume_kit.py`)

The 13 shop costumes are their own drawings (skin `sheetSet`, sheets in `<fighter>/<costume>/`): Soul Summoner ECLIPSE (sun corona, sunburst crown, torn gold-trimmed cape) / AURORA (ice crown, aurora cape); Mr Wake VAMPIRE (flared collar, bat-winged staff, scalloped cloak) / SAKURA (blossom branch on the hat, blooming staff); Lacy OBSIDIAN (lava-cracked glass armour, horns, obsidian blade) / CYBERBLADE (chrome + circuits, visor, neon blade) - then `hero_knight_moves.py` (its COSTUMES list) builds their moves; NU VAPORWAVE (gradient shades, sunset print); AERO SUNSET (sunset dome, goldfish in shades). Second wave: Soul Summoner CELESTIAL (`celestial`: starfield cape, silver star crown, crescent moon behind his head), Mr Wake PLAGUE DOCTOR (`plague`: leather beaked mask, green goggles, caged lantern on the staff), Lacy WILDWOOD (`verdant`: bark-and-moss armour, leaf crown, green wooden blade), NU GLAM (`glam`: platinum/pink hair, lightning bolt, glitter gold jacket), AERO MIDNIGHT (`midnight`: night-sky dome with a moon, goldfish in a nightcap). Hidden (RIPHOOD code): Soul Summoner OLIVE DRAB (`olive`: steel helmet with netting as an extra part over the head, crown removed, chest webbing). Each has a SKIN_INTROS arrival. Beach costumes (NU and AERO's arrival on the main roster, free): NU SURF ROCK (`surf`, `nu_surf`: open coral Hawaiian shirt with hibiscus over a white tank, turquoise board shorts, bare shins + flip-flops, aqua-tipped sun-bleached spikes, mirrored sunset shades, seashell mic), AERO TIDE POOL (`tide`, `aero_tide`: dome = a little tropical sea with sand, coral, a starfish and a clownfish, wave-striped white suit, beach-towel scarf, coral flippers and glove, scallop shell). Puppet fighters: parts recoloured + drawn on, moves re-rendered; Mason's hand-made idle/run get the changes grafted on (`costume_kit.graft`).
