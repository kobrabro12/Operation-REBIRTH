# tools/sprites/ — sprite generators

How fighter and costume art is generated from Mason's drawings.
The root `AGENTS.md` (rules, stack, map) applies too. Keep this file up to date when you change what it describes.

## Test fighters' legs
`legs_kit.py`: the shaped legs PRESTO, CHEF, THE DEVOTED, WHAT ONCE WAS and ROSEMARY share (they replaced two straight `limb` tubes drawn off the rig's joints, which popped apart at the knee). `seg` = a tapered segment drawn exactly between two rig pivots with a thigh / calf `bulge` and round ends, so thigh and shin overlap into one knee at any bend; `along` = points down a segment (stripes, seams, laces); `shoe` = a foot by kind (point / clog / boot / rain / loafer / bare). Each fighter's own look: PRESTO tapered tux trousers with a crimson satin stripe, tall white spats, long pointed patent shoes; CHEF baggy houndstooth with rolled cuffs, clogs on wooden soles; THE DEVOTED skinny jeans ripped above the knee and knee-high lace-up combat boots (`her_legs()` draws them in greys for `repaint`; laces and the skin through the rips are `MARKS`, put back after the repaint unless sludge covers them; the shin uses the SHOE ramp); WHAT ONCE WAS long gaunt wallpaper legs peeling in strips, mannequin ball-joint knees, scuffed office loafers (its bodies: suit trousers + dress shoes, SWAT knee pads + boots, scrubs + clogs); ROSEMARY slim calves in tights, red rain boots with a turned-down cuff and ridged sole (RED ARMY: khaki trousers, black jackboots). SEEN (her mascot form) keeps its own legs. After a change rerun the fighter's generator (`show_moves.py presto|chef`, `devoted_draw.py [--set murder|bride|ember]`, `what_once_was_moves.py`, `last_seen_moves.py --calm` / `--soviet`, `broken_bunny.py`) and its `mobile_atlases.py` entries.

## THE DEVOTED
`devoted_draw.py`: THE DEVOTED's four sludge-stage looks (repaints AERO's parts; sludge coverage `COVER`, smile per stage in `head()`, `wing()` = DROWNED's demon wing in the scarf slot, her own `idle_keys`) and her moves table `src/fighters/dvMoves.js`. Rerun it after changing the drawing. `--set murder` builds the MAD MURDER costume instead (`VARIANT`: `MURDER_SHIRT`, `BLOOD`, `bloody()`, `mask()`, a red blade) into `devoted/murder/`. `--set cartridge|bride|ember|cinder|heartwood|forge|keeper` build the other drawn costumes: `VARIANTS` (palettes, `facet` = flat low-poly shading, sludge ramp, wing colours, blade) applied by `apply_variant()`, plus their own touches in `vpaint()` (clothes patterns), `vhead()` (eyes, veil, halo, fire hair, CINDER WRAITH's hood and cracked mask - `HOOD` / `ASH`), `dress()` (skirt, bouquet, rose, rosary) and `knife()`; the three lore costumes add `lore_dress()` (branches + vine, leather apron + strap + hammer, buttons + rope + lantern) and `lore_wing()` (their own DROWNED wings), palettes `AMBER` / `IRON`+`LEATHER` / `BRASS`; FORGEBOUND's rolled sleeves are skin via `KIND`.
`devoted_knife_toss.py`: her TAUNT, the KNIFE TOSS (flicks the karambit up, it spins `SPINS` times along an arc up to `TOP`, she snatches it back): renders her twice (karambit in the fist / fist empty) and draws the knife cut out of `knife()` spinning between the release and catch points, into the `taunt` slot of her moves sheets (`FRAMES` = 90, one a tick, the slot dvMoves.js gives it - so no other frame moves). devoted_draw.py calls its `patch_sheet()` after each sheet; `--patch` redoes the taunt in every costume and stage's existing sheets, `OUT_DIR [--set C] [--stage N]` writes a strip + preview GIF. CARTRIDGE is skipped (its sheets are devoted_3d.py's 3D renders) and keeps the old taunt.

## AERO64
`aero_3d.py`: AERO's AERO64 costume as a real low-poly 3D model in Blender (needs `pip install bpy`), on top of devoted_3d.py (`Geo`, `palette_material`, `build_scene`, `animate3d`, `snap`, `shrink`; it swaps in its own `model()` and `place()`): glass fishbowl (`glass_material`: transparent middle, solid rim; the back of the bowl and the water are opaque so the outline doesn't punch holes), goldfish, suit, gloves, boots, leaf scarf; AERO's rig/keyframes/life from aero_moves.py -> `public/assets/fighters/aero/n64/` (moves `retile`d to 36 a row). Then `ONLY=aero_n64_moves python3 tools/sprites/mobile_atlases.py`. `--king` builds his FULL AERO64 (`king_model`: the same model with gold gloves/belt/soles, an aqua star on the chest, a royal cape with fur collar and gold hem in the scarf slot, a two-layer feathered wing with aqua tips, a gold halo (`torus`), the goldfish in a crown, the golden spear) on FULL AERO's rig (`aero_king_mason.source('full_aero.png')`, `KFOLLOW`, `king_life`, gold afterimages) -> `aero/n64/king/`, model `art/aero64_full.blend`; then `ONLY=aero_king_n64_moves python3 tools/sprites/mobile_atlases.py`.

## TROPHY HALL paintings
`hall_paintings.py` paints the 22 pictures in the TROPHY HALL (-> `public/assets/hall/paintings.png`, 5 to a row of 160x112, palette PNG; titles and stories in `PAINTINGS` in `src/data/trophyHall.js`): each `p_*` builds a background in code (Bayer-dithered sky bands `sky` / `bands`, `radial` glows, `ridge` silhouettes, rays, speed lines, impact `burst`s, sparkles) and stands the fighters in it from their real sheets (`strip` = a frame of an idle/run strip, `move` = a frame of a move, read from the generated `*Moves.js` tables; `put` trims and places by the feet, with a rim of the scene's light; `silhouette` for crowds/shadows), then `grade` (colour wash, contrast, vignette, grain). WHAT ONCE WAS uses `menu_idle_sheet.png` (its other sheets have no head). `--preview FILE` writes a 3x contact sheet. `hall_masterpieces.py` paints the four animated MASTERPIECES (320x224, 8 frames each -> `public/assets/hall/master_<name>.png`: `clash`, `feast`, `drowned`, `bowl`) on the same helpers (it sets `hall_paintings.W/H` to its size): nearer fighters at 2x, far ones at 1x for depth, clouds / waterfalls / embers / candles / steam / rain / lightning / waves moving with the frame number `f` (`ph(f)` = its phase), a fixed grain per piece (`finish`). `bowl` is a MOSAIC: `bowl_source(f)` paints a smooth picture of AERO's fishbowl, `tiles()` lays fixed Voronoi stones once (scipy `cKDTree`: square stones in the border, rings round the bowl), each stone takes the average colour under it with its own shade jitter, then a bevel, grout and glinting gold stones. Extra helpers in hall_paintings.py: `shop()` (a shopkeeper face or shop picture), `demon()` (THE DEVOTED's glutton / wraith / weeper, drawn small).

## GNASH
`gnash_draw.py` draws him part by part on NU's joints (`shade5`, five-tone `BASE`; `VARIANTS` = BLOOD MOON / TOXIC (folder `gravestone`)): Mason's street punk kid - a round black mask with neon X eyes and a neon zig-zag grin, dark spiky hair and two NEON HORNS (`horn()`: tapered solid neon with a white core) in the `hair` slot, ALL-BLACK clothes outlined in neon (`outlined()`: a black ramp whose last colour - shade5's outline ring - is the look's neon; `NEONS` = each look's outline / eyes-laces / diamond colours), and the GIANT axe in the `mic` slot. His parts are drawn on a 144x144 canvas (`PAD` = 40 px in; the body is drawn at 64 and `pad()`ed, the axe drawn straight onto the big canvas with `layer96` / `put96`; RIG pivots are +PAD): a long black haft with neon tape, held at the bottom, and a huge black spiked crescent blade with a glowing neon diamond outline in the middle. `gnash_moves.py` animates him: it swaps `N.build_rig` for `build_rig96` (strips 160x96 wrapping past 4096 px - show_moves.strip - and moves 180x168 at 22 to a row: `SMALL_CANVAS` / `BIG_CANVAS` / `COLS`; NU's normals at every 2nd tick - `half_steps`) and `N.life` for `gnash_life`, and every pose goes through `two_hands()` (in `gnash_life`): the back arm (shoulder moved mid-chest, its fist `bfist` drawn over the haft) is bent by two-bone IK, refined on the real rig with scipy, so the back fist lands on `GRIP` under the front fist - arms may stretch to `MAX_REACH`; skipped while the axe is thrown. His idle and run DRAG the axe behind him (`DRAG`, `gnash_idle`, `gnash_run` replaces `N.run_keys` while building), jump / fall haul it up behind his head (`air_poses`). NU's normals + his own specials, Limit and taunt from the poses `OVER` / `CHOP` / `LOW` / `HIGH` / `ROAR` / `SHOULDER` (for an axe overhead, bend the forearm back up: ffore +140..165). It builds everything twice: armed, then with `NOAXE_BUILD` (axe hidden, no grip, NU's arms, `noaxe_idle`) into `gnash/noaxe/[<costume>/]` for after AXE THROW. It also writes his icon and fixes gnMoves.js's header. The three MAP COSTUMES (`arcade` / `gravemoon` / `infected`) are VARIANTS with more than a palette: `horn` / `hornW` (the horns' material and width), `emblem` (`EMBLEMS`: a striped sun / a moon / a pixel heart drawn in the blade instead of the diamond), `glitch` (a cyan ghost of the horns), and `TOUCH[variant](parts, P)` drawing details onto the 64x64 parts (tee prints, mask scanlines / crack + fangs / crosshair eyes and a slipped scanline, joystick pin, belt lantern, bevelled collar; INFECTED's blade gets a title bar and close box). Each has its own idle and SEETHE (`skin_idle`, `skin_moves` -> show_moves.build's `own_for`; same lengths so one frame table fits all). POP LINES (`pop`) is the same kind of variant: flat ramps (four equal tones + a black outline, so shade5 draws no shading) and `emblem_pop` / `touch_pop`. NU's normals are fitted to the axe by `gnash_normals()` (called from `half_steps`): no smears (they drew big orange ghosts of the axe), `axe_up` flips the axe blade-up in the moves in `AXE_UP` (NU points his mic down at his mouth when he raises his arm, which turned the axe upside down), dash attack aimed forward (`aim(pose, degrees)`; `axe_angle` = which way the axe points, 0 up / 90 forward / 180 down), NAIR without NU's somersault (it spun GNASH right off his frame) - the axe whirls round him instead; UTILT is his own (an arc over his head). `CHOP`'s blade digs in at a steep angle; the moves frames are 180 wide (feet at 80) because every overhead chop swings the axe out flat for a frame or two. `GN_SETS=arcade,gravemoon python3 tools/sprites/gnash_moves.py` builds just those costumes. Then `ONLY=gn_moves,gn_bloodmoon_moves,gn_gravestone_moves,gn_arcade_moves,gn_gravemoon_moves,gn_infected_moves,gn_pop_moves python3 tools/sprites/mobile_atlases.py`.
