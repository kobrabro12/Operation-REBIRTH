# tools/sprites/ — sprite generators

How fighter and costume art is generated from Mason's drawings.
The root `AGENTS.md` (rules, stack, map) applies too. Keep this file up to date when you change what it describes.

## Test fighters' legs
`legs_kit.py`: the shaped legs THE DEVOTED, WHAT ONCE WAS and ROSEMARY share (they replaced two straight `limb` tubes drawn off the rig's joints, which popped apart at the knee). `seg` = a tapered segment drawn exactly between two rig pivots with a thigh / calf `bulge` and round ends, so thigh and shin overlap into one knee at any bend; `along` = points down a segment (stripes, seams, laces); `shoe` = a foot by kind (point / clog / boot / rain / loafer / bare). Each fighter's own look: THE DEVOTED skinny jeans ripped above the knee and knee-high lace-up combat boots (`her_legs()` draws them in greys for `repaint`; laces and the skin through the rips are `MARKS`, put back after the repaint unless sludge covers them; the shin uses the SHOE ramp); WHAT ONCE WAS long gaunt wallpaper legs peeling in strips, mannequin ball-joint knees, scuffed office loafers (its bodies: suit trousers + dress shoes, SWAT knee pads + boots, scrubs + clogs); ROSEMARY slim calves in tights, red rain boots with a turned-down cuff and ridged sole (RED ARMY: khaki trousers, black jackboots). SEEN (her mascot form) keeps its own legs. After a change rerun the fighter's generator (`devoted_draw.py [--set murder|bride|ember]`, `what_once_was_moves.py`, `last_seen_moves.py --calm` / `--soviet`, `broken_bunny.py`) and its `mobile_atlases.py` entries.

## LOADING SCREENS
`loading_screens.py`: the Fortnite-style loading screens (index.html styles them, `public/loading-screens.js` picks one per visit - never the same twice - and runs them; `?loading=<id>` forces one): per screen a 320x180 pixel backdrop `<id>_bg.png` painted in code (`bands` = dithered sky bands, `rays`, `glow`, `cloud`, `stars`, silhouettes; HANZ's is his `poly_room.png` shrunk) and a hero `<id>_hero.png` cut from the fighter's moves sheet in an action pose (`POSES`: sheet frame index; HANZ = hanz_fight.py's front `raise`; the roster = three founders), trimmed and outlined -> `public/assets/ui/loading/`. The page makes them move: the backdrop pushes in, light rays turn behind the hero, the hero slides in and floats with an offset shadow and glow in the screen's colours (`a` / `b`), particles per screen (`fx`: embers / souls / wisps / confetti / bubbles / rain / glitch) on a 320x180 canvas, the name, place and lore, turning TIPS, a slanted marching progress bar (BootScene fills `#loading-fill` / `#loading-percent`). The art is small and kept real (unpacked) in the claude.ai copy and precached by the service worker, so it shows before the game's code. Heroes come from clean poses (taunts / un-smeared frames - motion smears blown up looked broken), get a 1 px outline and a 2 px comic shadow baked on their own pixel grid (`shadowed`), and the page sizes each in whole/half steps of the backdrop's pixels (`px` in loading-screens.js: height = sprite rows x px / 180 of the screen), so sprite and backdrop pixels line up. A new fighter = a `bg_<name>()`, a `POSES` entry, a `SCREENS` entry, and a line in loading-screens.js (with its `px`).
`loading_stories.py`: STORY loading screens - a whole story told in one 320x180 picture (`story-<id>_bg.png`), painted with hall_paintings.py's kit at 320x180 (it sets `P.W/P.H`), every sprite at a whole-number scale: THEIR NAMES (Soul Summoner among his friends' graves, one ghost answering), WHAT THE WATER KEEPS (Mr Wake fishing off Gravemoon Bridge while giant eyes under the water reach for his line, AERO watching), THE LAST OATH (Lacy raising her sword above her burning kingdom), SOUNDCHECK, 6 AM (NU ready, GNASH asleep on the amp, AERO tangled in cables), THE STAR-EATER (an eclipse face swallowing the stars over three tiny heroes), CLOSING TIME (NU and AERO leaving the shop with bags while HANZ's two eyes watch from the window), SECOND LIFE (the six walking into the dawn). In loading-screens.js they're `story: true` (title, a STORY kicker, the tale as `lore`): the picture is framed whole in the top of the screen (`.ls-story`, a slow drift, no hero / rays / tip) with the title and story beneath. Run it after loading_screens.py (which keeps `story-*` files).

## HANZ / $@L (LOST)
