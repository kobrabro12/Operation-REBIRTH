VOICE STUDIO

Click RECORD, say the line like you're announcing a big match, then click STOP. Listen back with PLAY and record again until you like it — each new take replaces the last one. Every take saves itself into the project's recordings/voice folder. You don't have to do all of them: anything you skip keeps the computer voice.

When you've recorded the lines you want, tell Claude "my voice lines are recorded" and they'll be cleaned up and put into the game.
